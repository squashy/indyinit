Battle for the 92

Share one screen on Indiana’s 92 counties. A right answer adds 2 armies.

Set up

Choose 2 to 6 players. Type a name for each person or computer who is playing.

How you win
How to play
  1. Sit together at this screen. Play with 2 to 6 players. A player is a person or the computer.
  2. The game deals all 92 counties at random. You do not pick your counties.
  3. On your turn, the game picks one county you own and asks a question.
  4. A right answer adds exactly 2 armies, one time, before you place armies. A wrong answer adds 0. Skip adds 0. You still see the right answer.
  5. Place the new armies on counties you own.
  6. You may attack a county that shares a side with yours if someone else owns it. Touching only at a corner does not count. You roll up to 3 dice. The defender rolls up to 2. The higher number wins that roll. A tie goes to the defender.
  7. If you take a county, move armies in before you do anything else. Leave at least 1 army behind.
  8. Then you may move armies one time through counties you own, or you may skip that move.
  9. Pass the screen. A player with no counties is skipped.
  10. Quick: have at least 60 counties when your turn ends. Total: own all 92 counties.

The map shows who owns a county and how many armies sit there. “This step” tells you what to do now. Places in this county is for reading. It is not the quiz. The trip log writes what already happened.
