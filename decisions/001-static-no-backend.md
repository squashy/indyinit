# 001: Static site with no backend

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:** Plain static files on a CDN. No server, accounts or runtime AI calls.

**Why:** Our players are nine-year-olds, so collecting nothing is the right privacy position. It removes most of the attack surface and costs little to host, and a classroom spike is just cached files.

**Tradeoffs:** No online multiplayer, no shared leaderboards, and no adaptive questions across devices. Learning analytics would need a separate, privacy-reviewed add-on.

**When we would revisit:** A teacher dashboard or online play becomes a goal.
